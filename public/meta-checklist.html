The 5-step checklist to start running Meta ads

The exact math and setup I use before spending a dollar on Meta. Run your numbers below.

1

Know what you can afford

Your breakeven CPA is your contribution margin on one order. It's the most you can pay to acquire a customer and still break even on the first order.

AOV − COGS − shipping/fulfillment − (fees + avg refunds per order) = Contribution margin (breakeven CPA)

AOV ÷ breakeven CPA = Breakeven ROAS

You lose money on every first order at this price. Fix margin before running ads.

If customers buy again, first-order breakeven is a floor, not a ceiling.

2

Establish your starting budget

Meta recommends roughly 50 optimization events per week per ad set to exit the learning phase and stabilize delivery. Many advertisers still get solid results below that, especially on smaller budgets.

(Breakeven CPA × 50) ÷ 7 = Starting daily budget
